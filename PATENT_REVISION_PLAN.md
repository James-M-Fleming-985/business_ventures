## Preview: Strategic Outcomes Section (Example)

Here's how the corrected detailed description would explain strategic outcomes:

> **Strategic Outcomes Framework**
>
> The system optimises three user-configurable strategic outcomes measured across the architect's entire network of actor relationships:
>
> **Notoriety (N)** quantifies the degree to which relevant actors know, understand, and would support the architect's aims. The calculation aggregates three components for each actor: awareness (does the actor know who the architect is and what they do), understanding (does the actor accurately comprehend the architect's strategic aims), and support willingness (would the actor actively help if asked). Each component ranges from zero to one, with the product of the three values representing that actor's contribution to overall notoriety. The formula is:
>
> N = (1/n) × Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ (awarenesᵢ × understandingᵢ × support_willingnessᵢ)
>
> where n represents the total number of relevant actors in the network. A notoriety value approaching zero indicates the architect is unknown or misunderstood, while a value approaching one indicates widespread recognition and support.
>
> **Respect (R)** quantifies the degree to which actors feel understood, valued, and believe their wants, needs, and desires are considered by the architect. Similar to notoriety, this metric aggregates three components: feeling understood (the actor feels the architect comprehends their perspective), consideration (the actor believes their priorities are acknowledged), and value alignment (the actor perceives mutual benefit). The formula follows an identical structure with component values ranging from zero to one.
>
> **Financial Wealth (W)** quantifies economic value generation through salary increases, asset growth, cashflow improvements, opportunity pipeline development, and executed project value realisation. This metric comprises three sub-components: direct wealth (salary + assets + cashflow changes), pipeline wealth (sum of opportunity values multiplied by their respective execution probabilities), and executed wealth (realised gains from completed projects). Unlike notoriety and respect which normalise to zero-one range, wealth is measured in absolute currency units and can be optionally normalised for visualisation purposes.
>
> The system enables architects to assign priority weights to each outcome based on current strategic focus. For example, an architect pursuing career advancement might configure weights of w_N = zero point three, w_R = zero point two, w_W = zero point five, emphasising financial outcomes while maintaining moderate attention to visibility and relationships. An architect focused on relationship building might instead use w_N = zero point two, w_R = zero point six, w_W = zero point two. These weights directly influence the ROI Index calculation described in the strategic indexes section.
