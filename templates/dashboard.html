MAPE

Mean Absolute Percentage Error

Average magnitude of prediction error as a percentage. Measures how far off predicted values are from actual values, regardless of direction.

= mean(abs(actual - predicted) / predicted × 100)

Lower is better. Auto-validated daily at 02:00 UTC.

Target: <5%
